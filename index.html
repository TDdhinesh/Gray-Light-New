Born from a shared
passion for light.

“Two friends with cameras and a dream — Gray Light Studio was born on the streets of Omalur.”

What started as a shared passion between two friends has grown into a trusted creative studio. We bring together technical precision and artistic instinct to make photographs and films that feel lived, not staged.

From our home base in Omalur, Salem, we document weddings, portraits, brands and the quiet moments in between across Tamil Nadu.
